### Alex AN

Founder of [Anlan](https://anlan.ai), helping organizations and business teams put AI to work in real workflows—focusing on departmental workflow diagnosis, pragmatic tool selection, and human-AI collaboration redesign.

Open to conversations on departmental AI adoption and joint project delivery.
